# Review: YouTube demo page (web/youtube-demo)

Reviewer: Anusha · 24 Oct, evening

- Paris Cavanagh pieces are commercial/contemporary leaning contemporary; Saumya's is the fusion example: the describer
  should pick out the different style influences and use each style's technical language.
- Captions didn't match the speech and sometimes repeated (Ponyboy). Cause: an interrupted batch re-voiced Ponyboy under
  the same file names, so the page paired old captions with new audio. Fixed: audio named by a hash of its text.
- Narration took over the music, especially on Ponyboy (24 s, 70% speech in the first test). Fixed: ducking 45% -> 70%,
  ceiling 45% -> 40%, priority 1 narrowed.
- Track 10: "all the richness of the arms and sharpness is lost". Cause: the 35% cap cut "Waacking arm frames, sharp chest
  isolations" (priority 2) on a 41 s piece and kept generic lines. Fixed: the piece's signature is priority 1; in arm-driven
  styles describe the arms' path and dynamics; convey sharpness with callouts on the accents.
