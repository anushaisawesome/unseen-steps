# Anusha's feedback, 24–25 Oct (in her words)

A dancer's reactions to the narration across the whole night, kept as she said them (lightly tidied). This is what
Argon learns her taste from: what she valued, what she hated, and why.

## What good looks like

- "I would describe this motion in a specific way, at specific times, making sure to capture the expression, the
  emotion of the movement as well as what the pose is doing."
- On the first Argon-written lines: "I like these descriptions a lot better."
- "It's not reading a speech, it's tastefully providing commentary while allowing the music to remain the main
  reference point. Lines stay in meter and don't become the star of the show."
- "The speed of speech needs to be variable and emotive. If I was narrating the big part at the end with the chorus,
  I'd speed up to keep up with the music and describe each of the hits: 'Hit above the head, to the side, fold into
  the right!'"
- Track 10, an earlier version: "Some of them are good, especially before the climax. Towards the end is better,
  and I can hear it anticipating the climax too, which is good."
- Aquamarine: "70% there for quality, the only one I'm sort of happy with. It doesn't miss a lot. It needs more
  description of texture and pose, but it's almost there."
- Leiomy: "the description is nice"; hair and hands were caught.
- "The point is I want Gemini to have my dance eye, to be able to pick out the nuance from everything."

## What a dancer sees that was missed

- Ballet: "no mention of the moves she's doing. It missed the grand jeté, starting in fifth."
- "Is it gonna do anything richer than 'she spins and taps her hip'? Jumps and extensions mean something."
- Track 10: "all the richness of the arms and the sharpness is lost"; "literally nothing on the arm work, which is the
  main element of this choreo, and the sharpness"; "it loses the bit where the texture changes from sharp to fluid
  for the build-up at about 12 seconds. This needs to be picked up for styles I can't comment on as well."
- Track 10: "you miss the hits that make this choreo so good: that clap and turn round before the vocals come in."
- Judas: "missing the canons"; "it says nothing for an entire floorwork section."
- Ponyboy: "it missed the canon."
- Saumya: "lost her waacking early on, caught nothing on her Bharatanatyam posing for the second song" (a waacking
  and Bharatanatyam fusion). "I can see that, so the audience should be able to hear it."
- Leiomy: "missing the arm performance into a freeze, and the hand performance at the start"; "nothing on hair
  performance! She is constantly whipping her head."
- "Why aren't you watching legs and feet, for styles where they're important?"

## What was wrong

- "When you say plié it's often not a plié, it's just bent legs. A plié is bent legs with the knees and feet turned
  out. You use it for knees turned in, which is wrong. And you say second position when the feet aren't turned out."
- Judas: "it mistakes tutting for miming crowns"; "some of the motion language isn't accurate."
- Saumya: "said she was waacking rapidly when her arms were almost still."
- Ballet: "she's holding a tambourine, not a fan."
- Aquamarine: "where is the wide squat it's referring to? I'm not seeing it."
- Track 10's closing line "hips swaying into warm smiles": "warm smiles?" (not what the movement is; describe the
  groove).

## Delivery and timing

- Ballet: "far too slow, it's dragging, and over the music too much."
- "Lines are too slow, the timing is off, it feels late." Track 10: "late on the moves."
- "It's quiet for 10 seconds when a lot is happening."
- "Why is there so much silence and so many lines cut, they may as well not be there"; Ponyboy at 3 lines and a
  sparse Track 10.
- "This is turning into word salad"; "Leiomy's description is nice but the timing is all messed up, it's becoming
  less tasteful."
- "You still cut really good descriptive lines, especially for the groove at the end"; "the lines you kept compared
  to the ones cut are awful"; "it cut the first line, which is important context."
- Listening with eyes closed: "it feels different and not right, like it's not on beat."
- "It keeps saying the director's notes, so it doesn't stop talking"; "sometimes the voice doesn't say what the
  captions are saying."

## How to take this

- "Take my feedback generally. This tweaking isn't just for Track 10, but for other styles and videos too. Always
  find the root complaint."
- "Don't hardcode it, make the detection have more taste. I can see it because I have eyes, but don't make those
  who can't see miss out on it."
