Live venue

Describe a dancer, live

Point this device's camera at one dancer, whole body in view, like an audience member's view of the stage. Unseen Steps tracks the movement on this device and speaks a short description every few seconds. The video never leaves this device: only body measurements are sent to write each line.

Off by default, because the narration is already spoken aloud.

What it said

About every second and a half: what was measured, the line, whether it was spoken, and any draft the checker rejected. To stay live, a line that's ready too late for the movement it describes is skipped, not spoken late.

    At a real venue

    A fixed camera faces the stage and the description goes to each listener's own earphones, while the music plays from the room's speakers. "Scan the QR code" is a visual task, so a venue offers other ways in too: a short web address read out by the announcer, an NFC tag on the seat or programme, a link in the ticket email, and staff who can help.
